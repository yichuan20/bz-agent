# The Sky

Above the world, a canvas vast and wide,
Where morning light spills gold across the blue,
The clouds drift slow like thoughts that cannot hide,
And birds trace paths that only wind once knew.

At noon it burns with an unblinking eye,
A blazing dome of heat and summer haze,
While shadows shrink and eagles learn to fly
On thermals rising through the amber days.

By dusk it bleeds in crimson, rose, and flame,
The sun descends behind the distant hill,
And violet dusk lays softly down its claim
As the first star emerges, cold and still.

At night it holds a thousand silver lights,
A map of myths that ancient sailors read,
The moon, a lantern swinging through the nights,
A sky that comforts both the lost and freed.

So lift your eyes from all the weight below,
The sky has room for every grief and dream —
It swallows storms and still finds ways to glow,
And nothing ends quite as dark as it may seem.
